The Dungeons of Kollchap

Map viewer: torchlight, shadows, fog of war and materials (M1 in progress)

Map viewer controls

Path previewHover a tile (mouse) or tap it (iPad). The gold dots are the route from the party; the panel shows its length and the exploring time it costs.
WalkClick or tap the same tile again.
PanDrag, or WASD / arrow keys
ZoomPinch, or the mouse wheel
RotateTwist two fingers, Q / E, or ↺ ↻. N faces north again.
Whole map⤢ or F. ◉ goes to the party.
CancelRight-click or Esc
LightThe party carries a torch; the only other light comes from fires, the pool and daylight. You see what is lit and in view, and anything right beside you. Places you have seen stay as a dim memory; paths only cross explored ground. M turns on a map light and lifts the fog to see everything.
TogglesLow walls H, room tint R, labels L. Paths may use secret doors X (tinted purple), climb through pits P, and take the tunnel from room 9 to 18 T.

Tap anywhere to close.

WebGPU needed

This game renders with WebGPU. On iPad use Safari on iPadOS 26 or later. On desktop use a current Chrome, Edge or Safari. WebGPU also needs a secure page: localhost or HTTPS.

Graphics reset

The system reset the graphics device, which can happen after switching apps.
